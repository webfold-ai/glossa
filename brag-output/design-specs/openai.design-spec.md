# OpenAI Product-Marketing Motion — Design Spec

Status: reference incumbent spec. Format: RFC 2119 keywords (MUST, MUST NOT,
SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, MAY), written in ASD-STE100 style (short sentences, one
instruction per sentence, active voice, present tense, controlled vocabulary).
Scope: the motion and typography language of OpenAI's product pages and
launch pages (openai.com, ChatGPT/API launch pages).

## 1. Typography

1. A headline MUST use a humanist sans display face at regular or medium
   weight. A headline MUST NOT use a heavy or black weight as the default.
2. Tracking MUST stay close to neutral. Tracking MUST NOT go tighter than
   -0.02em at display sizes.
3. A headline SHOULD read as a calm, complete sentence, not a fragment.
4. Body copy MUST use the same type family as the headline. A second family
   MUST appear only for code or monospace content.

## 2. Color and Surface

5. A hero frame MUST use a near-white or near-black flat surface as the
   base. The base MUST NOT contain a busy pattern.
6. A soft gradient mesh MAY sit behind a headline. The gradient MUST use
   low-saturation, closely related hues. The gradient MUST NOT use more than
   three hues at once.
7. A glow or blur halo MAY surround one focal element. The halo MUST be
   subtle. The halo MUST NOT read as a light source with a hard edge.
8. Color MUST stay restrained. A scene SHOULD read as monochrome-plus-one
   accent, even when a gradient is present.

## 3. Motion and Timing

9. An entrance MUST fade in. A fade MUST pair with a small upward or inward
   drift, not a large slide.
10. An entrance MUST use a smooth decelerating ease. An entrance MUST NOT
    overshoot or bounce.
11. Motion MUST feel calm and unhurried. A scene MUST NOT use a fast whip-pan
    or a hard shake.
12. A gradient or glow MAY drift slowly and continuously across a hold. Drift
    speed MUST stay slow enough that it reads as ambient, not as an event.
13. A transition between scenes SHOULD be a soft crossfade. A transition
    MUST NOT be a hard cut on a loud beat.
14. A staged reveal (headline, then subhead, then detail) MUST use even,
    predictable spacing between stages. Each stage SHOULD land 0.4–0.8
    seconds after the previous one.

## 4. Composition and Pacing

15. A hero frame MUST center its primary claim. Off-center composition MAY
    appear only in a supporting or secondary frame.
16. Negative space MUST dominate. A hero frame SHOULD keep type/subject
    coverage under roughly 35%.
17. A scene MUST hold its settled state for at least 1 second before cutting
    away, to support a calm reading pace.
18. Numbers or claims MUST appear as plain statements. A number MUST NOT be
    escalated with color, size ramping, or a countdown effect.

## 5. What this style is NOT

19. This style MUST NOT use saturated, high-chroma color blocks.
20. This style MUST NOT use bouncy or elastic easing.
21. This style MUST NOT use dense multi-panel layouts in one frame.
22. This style MUST NOT use hard-edged geometric shapes as decoration.
